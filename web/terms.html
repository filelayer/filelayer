1. What each plan includes

Every paid service is delivered in writing: email, written reviews and security advisories. No plan includes calls or meetings.

Team, US$99 a month per organization, for up to 5,000 billable file owners across the organization's applications:

  • Email support from the Filelayer maintainers at support@filelayer.dev, with a target of a first reply within two business days. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding national public holidays in Spain, in Madrid time.
  • Priority on defects you report in the library: they are looked at before defects reported by people without a plan. Priority is about order. It is not a promise that a defect will be fixed, or by when.
  • Security advisories sent to the address you nominate before they are disclosed publicly. You agree to keep an advisory confidential, and to use it only to protect your own deployments, until it is disclosed publicly.

Business, US$299 a month per organization, for up to 50,000 billable file owners:

  • Everything in Team.
  • One review of your file model against the Filelayer adversarial suite in each 12-month period of the subscription. You send us, by email, a description of how your application uses Filelayer, and we reply with a written review.
  • A quarterly written architecture review. You send us, by email, a description of how you use Filelayer or questions about it, and we reply in writing. The target is to deliver it within 10 business days of your request. There is no call.

Enterprise, for more than 50,000 billable file owners: terms are agreed in writing, by email to enterprise@filelayer.dev.

3. Targets

The two-business-day reply time and the 10-business-day delivery time are targets. Missing a target is not a breach of these terms and does not entitle you to a refund or a credit.

4. Billable file owners

A billable file owner is a distinct owner with at least one live, ready file during a calendar month (UTC), counted per organization across its applications. A person a file is shared with is not its owner and is not counted.

You declare the number yourself, using the query published on the pricing page, run against each of your Filelayer databases. If your count is above your plan's limit for two consecutive months, you move to the next plan from the following billing month.

We may ask, at most once a year and with 30 days' notice, for the query results behind your declaration for the months in question. We do not access your systems to check. Anything you send us for this is confidential and is used only to check the declaration. If the count was above your plan's limit, you move to the right plan from the following billing month.
