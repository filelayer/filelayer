Pricing

You pay for support and reviews. The library is always free. It is Apache-2.0, with no limit on files, users or applications, and no charge for storage or bandwidth. Your files stay in your own bucket and your metadata in your own Postgres.

Every plan runs the same library, and nothing in it is held back for paying customers. The plans differ in what you ask of us.

PlanPriceForWhat you get
Free $0 Everyone The full library, every feature, in production. Questions and defects go to GitHub issues.
Team $99 a month per organization Small teams that want answers by email Email support with a target of a reply within two business days. Priority on the defects you report. Direct help applying a security fix.
Business $299 a month per organization Products with sensitive data, or customers who run security reviews Everything in Team, plus one review of your file model against the adversarial suite each year, and a quarterly written architecture review, with a target of delivery within 10 business days of your request.
Enterprise By email Your own contract, a data processing agreement or terms of your own Write to enterprise@filelayer.dev; there are no sales calls.

Security advisories are published to everyone at the same time. No plan receives them earlier; a plan gets direct help applying the fix.

As a guide, not a limit: Team usually fits an application with up to a few thousand people who own files, and Business one with tens of thousands. No plan counts or caps your usage.

How the paid plans work

  • Prices are in US dollars and exclude taxes. You can enter your VAT number at checkout. The plans are sold by Technology Pro Bono S.L., and payments are processed by Stripe.
  • Support for paying organizations is at support@filelayer.dev.
  • Monthly, per organization, and you can cancel at any time; the plan ends at the end of the month you have paid for.
  • Every paid service is in writing: email and written reviews. There are no calls.
  • The reply time and the review delivery time are targets, not guarantees.
  • Filelayer runs in your own infrastructure, so no plan carries an uptime commitment, and no plan includes a certification or a compliance attestation.
  • Paying adds nothing to the library itself. If something in Filelayer is free and open source today, it stays that way.
  • The full terms are at filelayer.dev/terms, and how we handle a buyer's personal data is in the privacy policy.

How much does Filelayer cost?

Nothing, to use it in production: the library is free and open source, Apache-2.0, with no limit on files, users or applications. You pay for support and reviews: Team at $99 a month per organization, Business at $299, and Enterprise by email.
